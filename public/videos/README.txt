Place your video files in this folder!

Default Video Path:
public/videos/our-movie.mp4

Format: MP4 (H.264 video + AAC audio works everywhere: Chrome, Safari, Android, iPhone).
When you place "our-movie.mp4" here, it will automatically play on Page 3 (Cinema of Memories).

If you want to use a different filename or add more videos, you can edit:
src/data/cinemaMemories.js
